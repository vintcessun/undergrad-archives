# 期末辩论

## PRIME MINISTER（首相）

### 我要做的事情：

1. 明确辩题定义，说明“国家测试”包含育儿知识、儿童心理、基本监护能力等内容
2. 陈述现状：当前部分父母缺乏科学育儿知识，导致儿童虐待、教育不当等社会问题
3. 提出三个核心论点：

\- 测试能提升父母育儿能力，降低儿童成长风险
\- 强制测试是对儿童权益的根本保护
\- 长期来看可减少社会福利支出（如儿童保护机构负担）

4. 总结论点逻辑，强调政策必要性

### 我要抄写在纸上的线索：

1. 定义：国家测试=育儿知识+心理+监护能力
2. 现状问题：育儿不当→儿童伤害
3. 论点1：提升父母能力
4. 论点2：保护儿童权益
5. 论点3：减少社会福利负担

### 发言稿范例：

​	Good morning, ladies and gentlemen, dear adjudicators and Mr. Speaker. This house believes that this house would require parents to pass a national test before being a parent. **For the purposes of this debate, we define the national test as including assessments on parenting knowledge, child psychology, and basic caregiving abilities.**
​	First, let’s look at the status quo. Currently, many parents lack systematic parenting education, leading to issues like child abuse and improper discipline. Studies show that 30% of child welfare cases are linked to parental incompetence.
​	**Now, my first argument is that such a test would enhance parents’ capabilities.** By ensuring they understand basic needs like nutrition and emotional support, we can prevent harm to children. **Second, this is a fundamental protection of children’s rights.** Children deserve parents who are mentally and skillfully prepared, not just biologically capable. **Third, in the long run, this policy would reduce social costs.** Fewer cases of neglect mean less strain on foster care systems and social services.
​	In summary, we propose this test to raise parenting standards, safeguard children, and build a more responsible society. That’s why we are proud to propose today’s motion. Thank you.

## LEADER OF OPPOSITION（反对党领袖）

### 我要做的事情：

1. 反驳首相的定义：指出“国家测试”可能缺乏科学标准，不同文化家庭育儿方式存在差异，测试内容可能主观片面
2. 反驳现状问题：强调多数父母通过家庭、社区自然学习育儿，育儿不当是少数极端案例，不能以偏概全
3. 提出三个核心反对论点：

\- 强制测试侵犯个人生育权，政府无权干预家庭内部事务
\- 测试无法真正评估“合格父母”，情感关怀、责任心等无法通过笔试衡量
\- 政策执行成本高昂，可能导致官僚主义（如测试机构腐败、资源浪费）

4. 总结反对逻辑，强调政府应提供教育而非强制考核

### 我要抄写在纸上的线索：

1. 反驳定义：测试标准主观→忽视文化差异
2. 反驳现状：多数父母合格→少数问题≠全民强制
3. 论点1：侵犯生育权→政府越权
4. 论点2：测试局限性→情感/责任心无法考核
5. 论点3：执行成本高→官僚主义风险

### 发言稿范例：

​	Ladies and gentlemen, we believe that requiring parents to pass a national test is a flawed and overreaching policy, and that is our stand. Before I show my arguments, I have several rebuttals against the other side.
​	**First, the prime minister defined the test as including parenting knowledge and psychology, but who sets these standards?** A one-size-fits-all test ignores cultural differences—for example, some families rely on extended family for childcare, which might not be reflected in a standardized exam. **Second, they said 30% of child welfare cases link to parental incompetence, but that means 70% of parents are doing fine!** We shouldn’t punish the majority for the mistakes of a few.
​	**Now let's come to my arguments.** **Firstly, this policy violates basic human rights.** People have the right to start a family without government approval; this is a dangerous step toward state control over personal choices. **Secondly, testing can’t measure what matters most—love, patience, and the ability to adapt.** A parent who scores high on a test might still fail to connect with their child emotionally. **Thirdly, think about the cost.** Creating a national testing system would require huge funding, and there’s a risk of corruption—what if rich parents bribe testers to pass?
​	In short, we oppose because this policy is an invasion of privacy, a flawed measure of parenting, and a waste of public money. Governments should offer parenting classes, not force exams. That’s why we are so proud to oppose today's motion. Thank you.

## DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER（副首相）

3. 总结政府方逻辑：测试是保护儿童的底线措施，而非限制生育权，本质是用制度托底家庭责任

### 我要抄写在纸上的线索：

1. 反驳LO定义：跨学科专家+文化弹性条款→标准科学
2. 反驳LO现状：X万例虐待→需事前预防
3. 论点1：弹性考核模块→兼顾多样性
4. 论点2：推动育儿文化→提升全民素养
5. 论点3：国际经验→降低犯罪+教育公平

​	In conclusion, our goal is simple: to protect children through reasonable, flexible standards that enhance parental capabilities without trampling on rights. That’s why we are proud to propose the motion today. Thank you.
