1. **Why didn't Bi Sheng's movable type printing become popular in China?**  

   I think there are factors like the complex character, the tradition and the cost that forbid the popularity of the movable type printing.

   Firstly, the complexity of Chinese characters, which are numerous and intricate, made the production and usage of movable types time-costing. 

   Additionally, the tradition, which the established woodblock printing technique was already deeply used and efficient for the time makes it difficult for a new method to replace it. 

   Finally, economic factors, including the cost of producing movable types and the limited market for printed materials, also contributed to its lack of widespread adoption.

2. **How did the division of labor in the production of porcelain contribute to its efficiency and quality?**  

   I think there are three parts of the division contribute to the changes: the specialization of person, the standardization of the process and the localization of the invention.

   Firstly, the division of labor in porcelain production allowed for specialized skills to develop, which significantly enhanced both efficiency and quality.

   Additionally, the standardization reduced production time and improved consistency in the final product, making the progress more precise.

   Finally, the localization of the invention facilitated innovation as workers could experiment within their areas of expertise, ensuring the continuous innovation.

3. **Can you explain the challenges faced by James Watt in improving Newcomen's steam engine?**  

   I think there are three parts of the challenges like the inefficiency of the machine, the hard condition of the running and the doubt from others.

   One major issue was the inefficiency of the engine due to its use of steam and cold water, which wasted energy. Watt had to develop a separate condenser to minimize heat loss, which was a complex engineering feat. 

   Additionally, he had to address the limitations in materials and manufacturing techniques of his time, ensuring that the engine could withstand higher pressures. 

   Finally, convincing potential users of the advantages of his improved design over existing technologies was another hurdle.
